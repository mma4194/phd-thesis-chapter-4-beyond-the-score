Scientific boundaries remain: no restored early full residential candidate discovery, independent sensor calibration, independent clock synchronization, independent residential deployment, or complete simpler-protocol comparator is claimed. Constructed development scenarios remain development evidence. Conditional intervals do not establish population-level generality.
